6. ZelfID Creation and Registration​

6.1 Creation of a ZelfID​

A User may create or obtain a ZelfID through the applicable Zelf interface, Protocol or registration process.

The creation or activation of a ZelfID requires the User to complete HumanAuthn, Proof of Humanity or other technical or security requirements established by the applicable Zelf Protocol.

6.2 HumanAuthn​

HumanAuthn is used as part of the enrollment and authentication process for a ZelfID.

During encryption, also referred to as the enrollment phase, HumanAuthn receives a live biometric sample from the User and independently generates high-entropy random material that contains no biometric features, biometric templates, cryptographic keys or feature vectors. This random entropy is stored for use by the HumanAuthn system.

Using the live biometric sample together with the stored random entropy, HumanAuthn derives ephemeral cryptographic key material that is used to encrypt identity metadata and generate a verifiable credential ("VC") designated as the ZelfID.

The HumanAuthn process is performed in a one-to-one (1:1) manner in relation to the specific ZelfID being created. It does not require searching or comparing the User's biometric sample against a database of other Users or previously created ZelfIDs.

The resulting ZelfID is designed to provide a privacy-preserving cryptographic identity credential without requiring Zelf to maintain a centralized biometric database, biometric template or feature-vector database for identifying Users or linking multiple ZelfIDs to the same individual.

The use of HumanAuthn does not give Zelf access to the User's derived private cryptographic material or control over the User's ZelfID.

6.3 ZelfID-QR​

A ZelfID is represented in two formats, the string resembling a user name and a portable QR-code format we name ZelfID-QR.

A ZelfID-QR may contain encrypted ZelfData and associated public metadata that may be used in connection with authentication, identification, encryption, decryption or other supported functionality.

The User is responsible for protecting any ZelfID-QR and any associated information that may enable access to or interaction with the User's ZelfID or Services.

6.4 No Traditional Custodial Account​

A ZelfID should not be understood as a traditional custodial account maintained by Zelf.

The creation of a ZelfID does not create a conventional account through which Zelf holds, manages or controls the User's information, credentials or Digital Assets.

A ZelfID is an independent cryptographic identity credential within the Zelf ecosystem. A User may create multiple ZelfIDs, and the creation of one ZelfID does not require or establish a link to other ZelfIDs created by the same User.

Unless the User voluntarily establishes such a relationship through the applicable Services, Zelf is not designed to maintain a centralized record linking multiple ZelfIDs to the same individual.

6.5 One User, Infinite Identities​

Zelf does not require a User to establish that they are a unique human being before creating a ZelfID. A User may create multiple ZelfIDs and may create as many identities as supported by the applicable Zelf Protocol, without being required to register or maintain a single, persistent identity with Zelf.

The creation of each ZelfID is based on a strict one-to-one (1:1) cryptographic process between the User's live biometric presence and the specific identity being created.

Zelf does not perform a one-to-many (1:N) biometric search or comparison against a centralized database of Users, biometric identities or previously created ZelfIDs.

Zelf does not maintain a centralized database containing biometric templates, facial feature vectors or other biometric identifiers for the purpose of determining whether a User has previously created another ZelfID.

Accordingly, the creation of one ZelfID does not prevent the same User from creating another ZelfID, and Zelf does not use HumanAuthn to determine how many identities a particular human has created or to link multiple ZelfIDs to a single underlying human identity.

Each ZelfID may therefore exist as an independent digital identity within the Zelf ecosystem without requiring a centralized record connecting it to other identities created by the same User.

This architecture is designed to preserve privacy and identity separation by allowing a User to create multiple independent digital identities without requiring Zelf to establish, maintain or disclose a single universal identity for that User.

6.6 User Responsibility for ZelfID​

The User is solely responsible for the security and use of each ZelfID and its associated authentication mechanisms.

Users must not knowingly allow another person to use their ZelfID or represent themselves as the holder of another person's ZelfID.

Because Zelf does not maintain a centralized biometric identity registry linking Users to their ZelfIDs, Zelf does not determine whether a User has created or controls other ZelfIDs.

Authentication is performed against the specific ZelfID being accessed in accordance with the applicable HumanAuthn and Protocol mechanisms.

The existence of multiple ZelfIDs does not, by itself, establish that those identities belong to the same User.

6.7 Loss of Access​

If a User loses access to the cryptographic materials, authentication mechanisms, device or other information required to use a ZelfID, Zelf may not be able to restore or recover the User's access.

Because Zelf does not maintain a centralized biometric identity registry or a universal identity linking a User to all ZelfIDs they may have created, Zelf cannot identify or restore a particular ZelfID by searching for the human who originally created it.

The User acknowledges that this limitation is a direct consequence of the self-custodial, zero-access and 1:1 architecture of the Zelf ecosystem.

6.8 ZelfID Is Not Government Identification​

A ZelfID is a digital identity credential within the Zelf ecosystem.

A ZelfID is not a government-issued identification document and does not, by itself, establish a User's legal identity, nationality, citizenship, residency, age or any other legal status.
